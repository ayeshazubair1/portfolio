About

My background in Economics has shaped the way I look at business performance: not just through the numbers, but through the factors and decisions behind them.

I'm interested in understanding how businesses operate, where performance falls short, and what the data reveals about the reasons behind it. Whether examining customer behavior, profitability, or operational performance, I approach each question with curiosity and a focus on getting the details right.

I work with SQL, Excel, and Tableau, with particular attention to data quality, validation, and sound analytical methods. My approach centres on asking the right questions, challenging assumptions, and presenting findings clearly enough to support better decisions

Questions I explore

  • Where is profit being lost, and what is driving margin erosion?
  • How much revenue depends on a small group of high-value customers?
  • Where is inventory disappearing, and how much capital is tied up in excess stock?
